# My Favorite Movies & Series

## The Wizarding World

![Hogwarts-style Castle](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1620054944439-d5d1c2fb643b?q=80&w=800&auto=format&fit=crop)

The Wizarding World encompasses the epic struggle against dark magic, spanning multiple eras of magical history from the 1920s to the legendary Battle of Hogwarts. 

### Harry Potter Series
Follows the journey of a young wizard, Harry, and his friends at Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry as they stand against Lord Voldemort.

#### Harry Potter and the Sorcerer's Stone
![Hogwarts Express](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1551269901-5c5e14c25df7?q=80&w=500&auto=format&fit=crop)
*An orphaned boy discovers he is a wizard and begins his magical education at Hogwarts, where he uncovers a dark secret hidden within the castle.*

#### Harry Potter and the Chamber of Secrets
![Snake Scales](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1533757705139-2ce181775eb9?q=80&w=500&auto=format&fit=crop)
*Harry returns for his second year, only to face a mysterious monster that is petrifying students and threatening the school's closure.*

#### Harry Potter and the Prisoner of Azkaban
![Full Moon](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1534330207526-8e81f10ece37?q=80&w=500&auto=format&fit=crop)
*Harry learns more about his parents' past when a dangerous fugitive escapes from the wizarding prison, Azkaban.*

#### Harry Potter and the Goblet of Fire
![Roaring Fire](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1577493340887-b7bfff550145?q=80&w=500&auto=format&fit=crop)
*Harry is mysteriously entered into the deadly Triwizard Tournament, forcing him to compete against older students and face a resurrected enemy.*

#### Harry Potter and the Order of the Phoenix
![Magic Sparks](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1517438322307-e6ce39f27170?q=80&w=500&auto=format&fit=crop)
*With the wizarding world in denial about Voldemort's return, Harry forms a secret student army to prepare for the looming war.*

#### Harry Potter and the Half-Blood Prince
![Potions and Vials](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1610461888750-10bfc601b874?q=80&w=500&auto=format&fit=crop)
*Harry uncovers Voldemort's dark past and the secret to his immortality while preparing for the inevitable final battle.*

#### Harry Potter and the Deathly Hallows – Part 1 (2010)
![Dark Forest](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1448375240586-882707db888b?q=80&w=500&auto=format&fit=crop)
*The trio goes on the run, abandoning Hogwarts to hunt down the Horcruxes that keep Voldemort alive.*

#### Harry Potter and the Deathly Hallows – Part 2 (2011)
![Castle Ruins](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1535905557558-afc4877a26fc?q=80&w=500&auto=format&fit=crop)
*The epic conclusion that brings the forces of good and evil together for a massive, final battle at Hogwarts.*

### 💼 Fantastic Beasts Series
Explores the earlier years of the magical universe, led by the eccentric magizoologist Newt Scamander and a young Albus Dumbledore.

#### Fantastic Beasts and Where to Find Them [2016]
![Vintage Suitcase](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1553531580-652231eb7067?q=80&w=500&auto=format&fit=crop)
*Magizoologist Newt Scamander arrives in 1920s New York, only for a magical suitcase mix-up to unleash magical creatures across the city.*

#### Fantastic Beasts: The Crimes of Grindelwald [2018]
![Gargoyle over Paris](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1502602898657-3e907600e628?q=80&w=500&auto=format&fit=crop)
*The dark wizard Grindelwald escapes custody and begins gathering followers in Paris to rule over all non-magical beings.*

#### Fantastic Beasts: The Secrets of Dumbledore [2022]
![Vintage European Streets](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1473220464492-452fb02e6221?q=80&w=500&auto=format&fit=crop)
*Albus Dumbledore entrusts Newt Scamander to lead a daring mission to stop Grindelwald's growing army and prevent a global magical war.*

## Middle-earth

![Middle Earth Landscape](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1464822759023-fed622ff2c3b?q=80&w=800&auto=format&fit=crop)

Based on J.R.R. Tolkien's legendary novels, these epic fantasy films explore the rich, mythical land of Middle-earth, filled with Elves, Dwarves, Hobbits, and Men.

### The Hobbit Trilogy
The prequel adventure detailing Bilbo Baggins' unexpected quest to reclaim the Lonely Mountain from the fearsome dragon Smaug.

#### 1. The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey
![Hobbit Door](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1506466010722-395aa2bef877?q=80&w=500&auto=format&fit=crop)
*A reluctant Bilbo Baggins is swept into a perilous quest by Gandalf the Grey and thirteen dwarves to reclaim the lost Dwarf Kingdom of Erebor.*

#### 2. The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug
![Gold and Treasure](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1620055610811-13c54452dc27?q=80&w=500&auto=format&fit=crop)
*The company journeys through the dangerous Mirkwood forest and Lake-town to finally confront the terrifying dragon, Smaug, inside the Lonely Mountain.*

#### 3. The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies
![Foggy Mountain Battle](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1465146344425-f00d5f5c8f07?q=80&w=500&auto=format&fit=crop)
*With the dragon defeated, a massive conflict erupts as Men, Elves, and Dwarves must unite to fight a devastating Orc army.*

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

### The Lord of the Rings Trilogy
The legendary, perilous journey of the Fellowship to destroy the One Ring in the fires of Mount Doom and defeat the Dark Lord Sauron.

#### 1. The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring
![Gold Ring](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1515259972304-453713068eaf?q=80&w=500&auto=format&fit=crop)
*A meek Hobbit from the Shire and eight companions set out on a journey to destroy the powerful One Ring and save Middle-earth from the Dark Lord Sauron.*

#### 2. The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers
![Stone Tower](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1500350917329-873b22e11894?q=80&w=500&auto=format&fit=crop)
*While Frodo and Sam edge closer to Mordor, the divided Fellowship makes a stand against the dark forces of Saruman at Helm's Deep.*

#### 3. The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King
![Volcano Fire](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1616423640778-28d1b53229bd?q=80&w=500&auto=format&fit=crop)
*The final, massive battle for the fate of Middle-earth begins as Frodo and Sam finally reach the treacherous slopes of Mount Doom.*
